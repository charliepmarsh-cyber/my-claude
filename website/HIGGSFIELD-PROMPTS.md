## Regeneration tips
- Keep `resolution: 2k` for full-bleed backgrounds (1, 7); `1k` is enough for inline cards.
- For social/OG crops, re-run the same prompt at `4:3` or `1:1`.
- For motion versions (hero video loop, LinkedIn b-roll), feed the finished image into
  Higgsfield image-to-video with: "slow cinematic push-in, data streams gently flowing,
  soft light pulses along the connections, seamless loop, no camera shake".
