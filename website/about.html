About

Built by an operator who ships systems

CPM Growth Systems is a UK-based growth systems studio founded by Charlie Marshall. One builder, senior-level output, AI leverage throughout.

The Founder

Charlie Marshall

Charlie Marshall is the founder of CPM Growth Systems. He builds AI-assisted growth, automation, CRM, lead generation and operational systems for ecommerce brands and businesses that need practical revenue infrastructure.

His background combines hands-on operations, AI tools, automation building, ecommerce systems and business development. That mix matters: most growth advice comes from people who have never run the operational side of a business. Charlie has — daily, physically, under time pressure. The systems CPM installs are shaped by that reality.

He works with a modern AI-native stack — n8n, Make, Zapier, HubSpot, Shopify, Apollo, Hunter.io, BuiltWith, Claude and OpenAI APIs — and builds everything to be documented, observable and owned by the client.

Operating Principles

How CPM works

1

Systems over services

You don't buy hours. You buy an installed, working system with a defined outcome and a handover.

2

Revenue first

Every build starts from the question: where does this create or protect revenue? If the answer is unclear, we don't build it.

3

Your stack, not ours

We build on tools you already pay for. No proprietary platforms, no lock-in, no hostage data.

4

AI-assisted, human-led

AI drafts, enriches, scores and reports. Humans approve, decide and own the relationship. That line never blurs.

5

Ship fast, iterate live

First working version in days, not months. Then tune against real data rather than debating hypotheticals.

6

Documented handover

Every system ships with docs and a walkthrough. If Charlie disappeared tomorrow, your systems would keep running.
